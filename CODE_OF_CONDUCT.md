# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want Icon Studio to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
publishing others' private information, and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the
project elsewhere. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break
these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at **harish.venkatesan23@gmail.com**. All reports
are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
